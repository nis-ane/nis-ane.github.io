Chaos in Harmony: Submerged Tale

Deep beneath the ocean, a grand temple stands tall—a symbol of peace and balance. Its strong structure represents harmony untouched by time. But all around, ruins tell a different story. Broken walls, fallen arches, and scattered remains show the chaos of destruction.

The water which was the cause of destruction that drowned the whole city now is still and calm. Among all the evidence of chaos of destroyed city the still water now represnts the harmony offering shelter to marine life. The corals, fish and turtles have found its place among those remains of city. Fish and turtle swim freely, gliding between harmony and ruin, unaware of the contrast

This is Chaos in Harmony—where beauty and destruction exist together, creating a world both broken and breathtaking. As there is a saying "Broken but Beautiful"
